# Changelog

## 1.6.0
- Updates install themselves: a new version downloads and is verified in the background, then installs after a 15-second countdown when nothing is downloading. "Later" installs it when you close the app.

## 1.5.0
- The version number is now always visible at the top of the app, and the app says it is developed by Garv.
- New one-line installers for Mac and Windows that avoid the "Video Downloader Not Opened" (Mac) and SmartScreen (Windows) warnings. See the README.
- The Windows app and installer now show Garv as the publisher in file properties.

## 1.4.0
- Renamed to Video Downloader. Your settings and sign-in carry over.
- New downloads go to a "Video Downloader" folder (Movies on Mac, Downloads on Windows) unless you picked a folder in Settings.

## 1.3.0
- Sign in to YouTube inside the app (Settings → YouTube account). Fixes "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" and age-restricted videos, without reading another browser's cookies.
- A browser-cookie setting that can't be read no longer breaks downloads; the app carries on signed out and says so.
- Windows: Chrome, Edge and Brave removed from the cookie options (Windows locks and encrypts their cookies); Firefox still works.

## 1.2.1
- Settings shows the installed version next to the update status.

## 1.2.0
- The app now updates itself. Every update is signature-checked and checksum-verified before it installs.
- Interface uses the mascot's blue; new app icon with the mascot.
- Faster launch: hardware encoder detection is remembered between runs.
- Download list updates in place instead of redrawing.

## 1.1.0
- Links from Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads and TikTok.
- Premiere Pro / After Effects check with one-click conversion to H.264, HEVC or ProRes 422.
- Jumping mascot on the link bar.
